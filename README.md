## Research Overview

This repository documents my master's research project on contextual stochastic optimization with decision-dependent uncertainty.

The project investigates how to learn and optimize decisions when the underlying uncertainty distribution depends on both contextual information and the decision itself.

## Research Objectives

The main objectives are:

1. Review the literature on contextual stochastic optimization, decision-dependent uncertainty, and performative prediction.
2. Develop a mathematical formulation for decision-dependent contextual optimization.
3. Investigate the relationship between performatively stable and optimal decisions.
4. Implement and compare prediction-based, repeated-retraining, and performative optimization methods.
5. Conduct reproducible numerical experiments.

## Literature Review

The research builds on several areas, including:
